How Auto-Debit Repayment Works
Your agreed monthly installment is automatically deducted from your registered ATM/debit card on the 28th of every month. You receive an SMS reminder on the 25th of every month — 3 days before each deduction. No manual payments needed — as long as your card has sufficient funds.
